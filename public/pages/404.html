Four mistakes we see most

Wrong depth

One level deeper than you think. The property pages live under public/pages/ and accounts under public/auth/.

Wrong case

File names are capitalised, including FAQ.html and Terms-of-service.html. Servers are case sensitive.

Dropped the id

Detail pages need their query, for example service-details.html?id=rental-watch.

Outdated bookmark

Some old dashboard links were retired. Go through the account menu instead of a saved link.
