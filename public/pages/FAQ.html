Short answers

The five we are asked most

How fast do you respond?

Median 27 seconds to verification, 95th percentile under 90 seconds, measured and published monthly.

Is dispatch ever billed?

No. Police, fire and medical dispatch is never invoiced, on any plan, at any time.

Are cameras included?

Yes, on every plan. Extra cameras are $8 each per month, and you keep them if you leave.

Do you keep my video?

Live view is never stored. Verified clips stay 48 hours by default, and you can shorten that or delete them.

Do you install the hardware?

Our Austin field team installs, and self-install is fine if you are comfortable with a ladder and a screwdriver.

Can I leave without a call?

Yes. Cancel from the dashboard, and we send one written confirmation. No retention call, no dark pattern.

Before you ask

Three things that speed things up

Most delay in activation comes from missing paperwork, not from us being busy.

    Proof you own or manage the property

    A utility bill, lease, or the settlement page from your closing. Two pages is plenty.

    Your city alarm permit number

    If your municipality requires one, the desk cannot activate without it. We tell you in the first reply.

    A real phone number for alerts

    Not a VoIP landline you are moving away from. This is the number we call when something happens at 3am.
