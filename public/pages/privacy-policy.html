Privacy Policy

Cameras inside your home are sensitive by definition. This policy explains every piece of data VaultGuard holds, why we hold it, and how long it stays.

1. Who we are

VaultGuard Security Inc. is a security monitoring company registered in Texas, United States, with its monitoring operations centre at 2400 South Lamar Blvd, Suite 300, Austin, TX 78704. When this policy says "we", "us" or "our", it means VaultGuard Security Inc. Data protection contact: privacy@vaultguard.com.

This policy covers the VaultGuard website, the resident and host dashboards, the VaultGuard app, our cameras, doorbells, sensors and smart locks, and any support handled by our staff.

2. What we collect

2.1 Video and audio from your devices

When you subscribe to a monitored plan, your cameras and doorbell upload encrypted video clips and still images. Microphone audio is disabled by default on every device we sell. If you enable two-way talk, audio is transmitted only while you are speaking or listening.

2.2 Event and sensor data

Motion events, door and window contact states, keypad and lock codes, arm and disarm actions, device identifiers, and the resulting verification decisions are recorded as part of the security timeline.

2.3 Account and billing data

Name, email, phone, property address, plan, payment method token, invoice history, permit registrations and support correspondence. Card details live with our payment processor; VaultGuard stores only a token and the last four digits.

2.4 Technical data

Device model, firmware version, IP address, time zone, signal quality, app version, crash logs, and cookie-based preferences such as theme and text direction.

Your rights

Access, correction, export and deletion all live in the dashboard. No form, no request fee, no waiting.

3. Why we use it, and on what basis

We process this data to perform the monitoring service you purchased, which is necessary to deliver the contract and to meet our obligations as a licensed alarm company. Specifically, we use video to verify events, sensor data to raise alerts, and contact data to reach you and your authorised contacts during an incident.

We also use aggregate, non-identifying data to tune detection and reduce false alarms. Marketing email is sent only where you have opted in, and every message carries a one-click unsubscribe. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not use your footage to train third-party models.

4. Who can see your data

  • Our operators. Only while handling an alert at your property, and recorded in the event log with their name.
  • Your authorised contacts. You choose who receives alerts, verification clips and cancellation confirmations.
  • Service providers. Payment processing, SMS delivery and cloud hosting, each bound by contract to our standards.
  • Law enforcement. Only with a valid legal process, and we notify you unless the law forbids it.

Default retention

  • Live view: never stored
  • Verification clips: 48 hours
  • Incident reports: 12 months
  • Invoices: 7 years

Shorten it yourself

Set a window of 24 hours, 48 hours, 7 days or 30 days in dashboard settings. Older clips are deleted automatically.

6. Your rights and controls

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. VaultGuard gives you all of this without a formal request:

  • Export. Download every clip, report and invoice as a ZIP archive from your dashboard, at any time.
  • Delete. Set a retention window and permanently remove older clips whenever you like.
  • Correct. Update name, phone, contacts and notification schedules yourself in settings.
  • Withdraw consent. Turn off marketing and non-essential analytics from the same page.

Deleting your account cancels monitoring immediately. We keep only what we are legally required to retain, and stop processing footage.

Report a data incident

Write to security@vaultguard.com. We acknowledge within 24 hours and keep you informed until it is closed.

Safeguards

  • TLS 1.3 in transit
  • AES-256 at rest
  • Role-limited staff access

7. Shared properties, guests and household members

If you use Smart Rentals, guests receive a one-time access pass and a limited set of arrival details. Guests do not get an account, cannot view history, and their access ends when the booking ends.

If several adults share a home, each may be an account holder with their own credentials, and each sees only what their role permits. Every operator action and every remote lock change is attributed to a named account in the event log.

8. Security safeguards

Video is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Device credentials are provisioned individually and rotated on every re-pair. Staff access to customer data is logged, limited by role, and reviewed quarterly. Our operator floor is badge-controlled and the operators themselves are on camera.

We test recovery procedures twice a year. Backup copies are encrypted with separate keys and are never used for anything but restoring the service.

9. Children and third parties

VaultGuard is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. We have never sold data and we do not sell data now.

Third parties such as our payment processor, SMS provider and cloud host process data only on our documented instructions, and each is contractually bound to the standards described here.

10. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our processing changes. Material updates are announced in the dashboard at least 30 days before they take effect, and the updated date is shown at the top of this page. Superseded versions are available on request.

Questions, requests or complaints: privacy@vaultguard.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact your local data protection authority.
