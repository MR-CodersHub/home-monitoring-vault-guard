Money questions

How billing actually works

When am I charged?

Monthly plans bill on the day you subscribed. Annual plans bill once for the year. Nothing else is ever added at checkout.

Are permit fees included?

Alarm permits are billed by your city and are not part of our fee. We remind you before renewal so registration never lapses.

Can I switch plans?

Any time, from your dashboard. Upgrades are prorated to the day, downgrades apply from your next billing date.

What happens if I cancel?

Monitoring stops at the end of the paid period. We keep your event archive for 30 days in case you need an incident report.
