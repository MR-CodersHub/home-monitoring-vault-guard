Our story

It started with one unanswered alarm

In 2015 a break-in at a family house went unanswered for 40 minutes because the monitoring contract had quietly moved overseas. The family had cameras and an app, and still nobody came.

VaultGuard was built so that cannot happen: one desk, in Austin, staffed around the clock, with every action written into a timeline the customer can read afterwards.

How we decide

Four rules the team actually follows

Verify before escalating

No authority is contacted on an unverified event unless you pre-authorise fire, medical or duress.

Publish the numbers

Response time, false alarm rate and incident retention are on this site, not buried in a report.

Keep the desk in-house

Every operator is trained, background-checked and answerable by name in the event log.

One price, no add-fees

Cameras included. Dispatch included. No per-alert billing, ever.
