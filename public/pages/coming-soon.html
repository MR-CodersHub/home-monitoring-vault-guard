How it works

Three decisions, not a hundred

The mesh only shares with neighbours you explicitly approve, only during windows you set, and only after verification.

Choose your circle

Approve by doorbell or verified address. No open network, no strangers, no marketplace.

Set your window

Share motion clips during daytime only, night alerts only, or the full event feed. Per-neighbour rules.

Verified, not raw

Neighbours receive verification tags, not your raw archive, and they cannot download or forward anything.

    Built for

    Who the mesh is actually for

    If nobody on your street will share cameras with you, the mesh helps you do not need it.

    Good fit

    Ground floors and entrances, buildings with a friendly neighbour, hosts with a driveway camera, anyone who already owns a smart lock.

    Poor fit

    Shared gates you cannot open from a button, buildings where cameras are forbidden by lease or law, anyone wanting footage on request.

    Not yet

    Multi-storey access control and any scheme that needs police-side integration. Both are on the roadmap, neither ships in the beta.
