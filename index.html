How it works

A sensor trips. A human decides.

Automation catches the event, our operators confirm it on live video, and only then does anything escalate. That single decision is why our false alarm rate sits at 0.3%.

Detect

Door contact, motion, glass break or smoke. The panel signals over cellular or Wi-Fi within seconds.

Verify

An operator pulls the camera clip, checks the scene and calls you if anything looks unusual.

Respond

Pre-authorised fire and medical alerts dispatch immediately. Everything else escalates on your instruction.

Report

You get a timeline with every action taken, the operator name and any footage kept for 90 days.
